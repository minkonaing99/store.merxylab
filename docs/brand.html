muted fails AA for body text at 3.32:1, and the site uses it at 12–13px for field labels, helper text and metadata. An earlier DESIGN.md recorded 4.6:1, which was wrong. Darkening to about #6F685C clears 4.5:1 while staying in the same warm family. Until then, treat muted as decorative weight and keep nothing load-bearing in it.

accent at 3.63:1 is fine for buttons, focus rings and 24px-plus headings, but not for small links or body copy. Use ink with an accent underline, which is what the site does.

Voice

06

On brand

Nothing on the shelf is filler.

Featherweight esports shells and full-size workhorses. Honest shapes, sensors that hold their aim.

We will call to confirm this order within 3 hours. Pay the courier in cash on delivery.

Real product in a sealed box, with the manufacturer warranty intact. No grey-market surprises.

Off brand

Unleash your true potential with GAMING GRADE precision!!

Built by hand in small batches.

Chosen for the desk, not the tournament.

We’ll be in touch shortly.

Plain, specific, unhurried. Short declaratives, and a period at the end of a headline is a house habit. No em dashes, no exclamation marks, no hype adjectives. Never claim to manufacture. Never position against gaming: the shop sells an esports mouse, it just does not shout about it.

Imagery

07

Prompt toward

Warm cream seamless backdrop, soft diffused window light from one side, matte surface, shallow grounded shadow, editorial product photography, muted warm palette, subject filling 60–75% of frame, no text, no logos.

Prompt away from

Neon, RGB lighting, dark moody studio, lens flare, glossy black acrylic, blue rim light, gaming setup with LED strips, motion blur, people, pure white backdrop, gradients.

Files: WebP, quality 82, 1600px long edge, under 200KB. Square 1:1 for grid tiles, 4:5 for hero, so a photo replacing a swatch does not shift the layout. Slots per product: 01 hero, 02 detail, 03 angle, 04 in-context.
